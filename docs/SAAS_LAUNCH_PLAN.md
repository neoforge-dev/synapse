# Synapse Graph-RAG SaaS Launch Plan

## 1. Pricing Tiers

We propose a three-tier pricing model designed to cater to individual developers, small to medium-sized teams, and large enterprises.

### **Individual (Solo Innovator)**
*Target: Hobbyists, Freelancers, Early-stage Startup Founders*
*   **Price:** $29 / month
*   **Core Value:** Affordable access to powerful Graph-RAG capabilities for personal projects or MVPs.
*   **Limits:**
    *   1 User
    *   Up to 100,000 nodes/edges
    *   5GB Vector Storage
    *   Standard Support (Community/Email)

### **Team (Growth Accelerator)**
*Target: Startups, SMBs, Research Groups*
*   **Price:** $149 / month (includes 5 users, +$20/additional user)
*   **Core Value:** Collaboration features, higher limits, and priority performance for growing applications.
*   **Limits:**
    *   Includes 5 Users
    *   Up to 1,000,000 nodes/edges
    *   50GB Vector Storage
    *   Shared Knowledge Graph Workspaces
    *   Priority Email Support

### **Enterprise (Scale & Security)**
*Target: Large Corporations, Regulated Industries*
*   **Price:** Custom Pricing (Contact Sales)
*   **Core Value:** Unlimited scale, advanced security/compliance, and dedicated infrastructure.
*   **Limits:**
    *   Unlimited Users & Roles (SSO)
    *   Unlimited nodes/edges (Custom Infrastructure)
    *   Private Cloud / On-Premise Deployment Options
    *   Audit Logs & Compliance Reporting (SOC2, HIPAA ready)
    *   Dedicated Success Manager & 24/7 SLA Support

## 2. Trial Structure

To drive adoption and reduce friction, we will offer a robust trial experience.

*   **Duration:** 14-Day Free Trial (No Credit Card Required).
*   **Access Level:** "Team" tier features (to showcase collaboration capabilities).
*   **Onboarding:**
    *   Automated "Hello World" walk-through upon sign-up.
    *   Pre-populated demo dataset (e.g., a sample knowledge graph of a wikipedia subset).
*   **Conversion Strategy:**
    *   Day 3: "Did you know?" email highlighting advanced query features.
    *   Day 7: Case study showcase.
    *   Day 12: "Trial ending soon" alert with a 20% discount offer for the annual "Individual" or "Team" plan.
*   **Post-Trial:** Downgrade to a "Free Tier" (highly restricted: read-only or <1000 nodes) or account pause.

## 4. Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

### **Phase 1: Soft Launch (Weeks 1-4)**
*   **Focus:** Feedback loops and stability.
*   **Channels:**
    *   Direct outreach to existing beta users on GitHub/Discord.
    *   "Invite Only" access for high-intent waitlist members.
*   **Content:** Technical documentation polish, migration guides.

### **Phase 2: Public Launch (Month 2)**
*   **Focus:** Awareness and User Acquisition.
*   **Channels:**
    *   **Product Hunt Launch:** Coordinate community support.
    *   **Content Marketing:** "Building a Knowledge Graph in 5 Minutes" blog series/video.
    *   **Social:** Twitter/LinkedIn threads dissecting "Why traditional RAG fails (and how Graph-RAG fixes it)."
*   **Offer:** "Founding Member" badge and lifetime discount for first 100 subscribers.

### **Phase 3: Growth & Partnerships (Month 3+)**
*   **Focus:** Scaling and Ecosystem integration.
*   **Channels:**
    *   **Partnerships:** Integration plugins for LangChain, LlamaIndex.
    *   **Webinars:** Deep dives into specific industry use-cases (Legal, BioTech).
    *   **Sales:** Outbound sales team activation for Enterprise leads.
